Privacy Policy

This policy covers the iOS apps Daily Verse, Daily Quran and Daily Tanakh (the "Apps"), published by Dana Riecken.
Last updated: October 23, 2026

We don't collect your data

The Apps don't collect, store, transmit, sell or share any personal information. They have no accounts, advertising, analytics, crash reporting or tracking, and they don't use third-party SDKs.

Information stored on your device

Your favorites, reminder time and app settings are saved only on your device, in storage shared between each App and its widget. The developer never receives this information. Deleting an App deletes it.

Notifications

Daily reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. No server is involved. You can turn them off in the App or in iOS Settings.

In-app purchases

The optional Premium unlock is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store (StoreKit). Apple handles payment details under Apple's Privacy Policy, and the developer receives no personal or payment information. The App checks with Apple, on your device, whether you own Premium.

Sharing

When you share a verse, the iOS share sheet sends it only to the destination you choose.

Children

The Apps collect no data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
