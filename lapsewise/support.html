Frequently asked questions

I'm not getting reminders. Open Settings in Lapsewise and make sure Renewal reminders is on, then check that notifications for Lapsewise are allowed in the iPhone Settings app. Reminders arrive at 9:00 AM on the chosen day before each charge.

How many subscriptions can I track? Up to 5 for free. Lapsewise Pro (a one-time purchase, not a subscription) unlocks unlimited subscriptions, reminder timing up to 30 days ahead, and CSV export.

I bought Pro but it isn't unlocked. Open Settings in Lapsewise and tap Restore purchase, signed in with the same Apple ID you bought it with.

How is the monthly total worked out? Weekly, quarterly and yearly prices are converted to a monthly amount. Free trials count as $0 until they convert.

Is my data backed up or synced? Your list stays on your iPhone and isn't synced to other devices. Deleting the App deletes it, so export a CSV (Pro) first if you want a copy.

Refunds: purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
