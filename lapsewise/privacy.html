Lapsewise Privacy Policy

Effective October 25, 2026

Lapsewise ("the App") is a subscription and free-trial tracker for iPhone made by Dana Riecken (Cadence Films Inc). The App does not collect any data. There is no account, no sign-in, no bank connection, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and the developer runs no servers that the App talks to.

Information stored on your device

The subscriptions you enter (name, price, currency, billing cycle, category, first bill date, trial status, reminder timing and notes) are stored only on your iPhone in the App's local database. Two settings (whether reminders are on, and whether you have seen the welcome screens) are stored on your device too. The App does not sync this data to iCloud or any other server. Like other app data, it may be included in your iPhone's own iCloud or computer backup if you have backups turned on. Deleting the App deletes this data.

Reminders

If you allow notifications, the App schedules local notifications on your iPhone before each renewal or trial end. They are created on your device and are not sent through any server run by the developer.

CSV export (Pro)

If you use Export CSV, the App creates a file of your list on your device and opens the iOS share sheet. The file only goes where you choose to send it.

In-app purchase

Lapsewise Pro is a one-time purchase handled by Apple through the App Store. The App checks with Apple whether you own Pro. The developer does not receive your payment details or Apple ID. Apple's handling of purchases is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

The App does not collect any data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
