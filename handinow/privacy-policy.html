Privacy Policy

This policy covers the iOS app Handinow (the "App"), published by Dana Riecken. Last updated: October 24, 2026

Current version

The current version of the App runs in demo mode with sample pros and mock payments. It doesn't send your information to the developer or to any third party, and it has no advertising, analytics or tracking.

Information stored on your device

The name, email and phone number you enter during setup, your job requests, messages and settings are stored only on your device. Signing out or deleting your account in the App removes them; deleting the App deletes them.

Photos

Photos you choose with the iOS photo picker are analyzed on your device with Apple's Vision framework to suggest a job category. They aren't uploaded.

Location

The current version doesn't access your device location.

Face ID

Face ID confirms bookings through iOS. The App never accesses or stores biometric data.

If live booking is added

If the App later connects to a live service, booking a job will require sharing your name, contact details, job address, job notes, photos and messages with the pro who accepts the job and with service providers such as payment, identity and background-check vendors. This policy will be updated before that happens.

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
