Frequently asked questions

What jobs can I book? Approved categories only: handyman repair, painting, furniture assembly, TV and wall mounting, yard work, snow removal, small deliveries, cleaning, minor and major plumbing, electrical, and gas appliances.

Why does the App ask for Face ID? Every booking is confirmed with Face ID so nobody can book from your phone without you.

Why do I see "Demo mode"? The App is showing sample pros and mock payments. No real money is charged and no real pro is sent.

How do I report a problem with a job? Open the job and tap the flag icon, or use the safety button.

How do I delete my account? Go to Account › Delete account and confirm with Face ID.
