1. The service

Voltri is a marketplace that helps customers find local pros for approved repair and errand tasks. Voltri is not the employer of pros and does not perform the work. The contract for the job is between the customer and the pro.

2. Accounts

You must provide accurate information, keep your sign-in credentials secure, and be at least 18 years old (or the age of majority where you live) to use Voltri. You may delete your account in the App or by emailing driecken@icloud.com.

3. Approved tasks only

Only categories listed in the App may be booked. Notes and photos are screened for prohibited content (for example unsafe work, illegal requests, or attempts to exchange personal contact or payment handles outside masked chat). Electrical, gas and major plumbing jobs are matched only with pros who have a verified license on file. Voltri may refuse, cancel or hold any request for review.

4. Booking fee and pro payment

When a customer confirms a booking, Voltri charges a 15% booking fee on the job price through PayPal. The customer pays the pro directly for the job itself. Voltri does not take a cut of the pro's price and does not hold the pro's earnings. The booking fee is refunded if the customer cancels before the pro is on the way. Chargebacks, disputes and fraud may be investigated and may lead to account suspension.

5. Cancellations and completion

Cancellation is free until the pro is on the way. After the job, both sides confirm completion (or raise an issue). If nobody raises an issue, funds related to the booking fee follow the App's stated release rules (including automatic release after the published waiting period when applicable).

6. Safety and conduct

Treat others respectfully. Do not harass, discriminate, or request or offer illegal services. Use the in-app Report tools and SOS features when needed. In an emergency call local emergency services (911 in the U.S.).

7. Ratings and reports

Ratings are two-way and may be held blind until both sides submit or a waiting period passes. Report & refuse on offers carries no acceptance-rate penalty. Repeated credible reports may lead to review or suspension.
