Privacy Policy

This policy covers the iOS app Voltri: Local Repair & Errands (the "App"), published by Dana Christian Riecken.
Last updated: October 31, 2026

What Voltri does

Voltri connects customers with local pros for approved repair and errand tasks. Customers describe a job, see a flat estimate, book a pro, and pay Voltri a booking fee through PayPal. The job price is paid by the customer to the pro directly (for example cash, Venmo, Zelle or a card reader). Voltri does not hold the pro's earnings.

Information we collect

  • Account: name, email, and (for phone masking) a phone number you verify.
  • Profile and jobs: role (customer or pro), neighborhood-level location until a booking is accepted, job details you enter, optional photos of the task, chat messages about a job, ratings and reports.
  • Device sign-in: a device public key used for secure device login (no password is stored by Voltri).
  • Payments: when you pay the Voltri booking fee, PayPal processes the payment. Voltri receives confirmation of the fee amount and payment status, not your full card number.
  • Pros: optional trade-license information you upload so licensed categories can be matched correctly, and approximate location while you are online to receive offers.

How we use information

We use this information to run matching and booking, mask calls and chat, process the booking fee, prevent fraud and prohibited tasks, provide support, and improve safety. Exact addresses are shared with a pro only after they accept a job, and are hidden again when the job is done.

Sharing

  • Pros and customers on a job see what they need to complete it (task details, neighborhood then address after accept, masked chat/call).
  • PayPal processes booking-fee payments under PayPal's Privacy Statement.
  • Infrastructure providers that host the Voltri API (currently Render) process data to keep the service running.
  • We do not sell personal information. We do not use third-party advertising or cross-app tracking SDKs.

Retention and deletion

By default, chat and job photos can be deleted 30 days after a job when that setting is on. You can request a copy of your data or delete your account from the App (Account → Privacy) or by emailing driecken@icloud.com. Some records may be kept longer when required for disputes, legal obligations or fraud prevention.

Children

Voltri is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Security

We use industry-standard protections in transit (HTTPS) and device-bound sign-in keys. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
