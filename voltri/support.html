Frequently asked questions

How does payment work?

You pay your pro directly for the job (cash, Venmo, Zelle, card reader, or another method you agree on). Voltri charges a separate 15% booking fee through PayPal when you confirm with Face ID. The fee is refunded if you cancel before your pro is on the way.

Why PayPal instead of Apple In-App Purchase?

Voltri arranges real-world services between people. Under App Store Guideline 3.1.3(e), goods and services experienced outside the app may use purchase methods other than in-app purchase. The booking fee goes to Voltri via PayPal; the pro is paid directly.

What tasks can I book?

Only the approved categories listed in the app (handyman repairs, assembly, TV mounting, painting, cleaning, yard and snow work, small deliveries, and licensed electrical, gas and major plumbing). Unsafe or illegal requests are blocked. See the Approved-Task Policy.

How do I switch between customer and pro?

Open Account and use the Mode control. Pros complete a short verification checklist before accepting offers.

How do I delete my account or download my data?

In the App: Account → Privacy. Or email driecken@icloud.com.
