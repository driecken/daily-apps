Frequently asked questions

How is the verse of the day chosen?

Each calendar date maps to one passage in a fixed rotation, so everyone sees the same passage on the same date. It changes at midnight in your time zone.

How do I add the widget?

Touch and hold the Home Screen, tap + (or Edit → Add Widget), search for the app's name, and choose a size. For the Lock Screen, touch and hold the Lock Screen, then tap Customize.

I'm not getting reminders.

In the App, open Settings and turn on Daily Reminder. Then check iOS Settings → Notifications → the App and make sure notifications are allowed. Open the App at least once a month so it can schedule more reminders.

How do I turn off the red badge?

In the App, go to Settings and turn off Show badge on app icon, or turn off Badges in iOS Settings → Notifications → the App. Opening the App clears the badge.

What does Premium include?

Premium is a one-time lifetime purchase, not a subscription. It unlocks unlimited favorites; the free version saves up to 10. Everything else is free.

How do I restore Premium on a new device?

In the App, go to Settings → Premium → Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple ID.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Which translations are used?

All texts are in the public domain, and each App's About screen lists its sources:

  • Daily Verse: the World English Bible, Reina-Valera 1909, 和合本 (1919), 文語訳聖書 and Almeida 1911.
  • Daily Quran: Pickthall's The Meaning of the Glorious Koran (1930).
  • Daily Tanakh: the Westminster Leningrad Codex, JPS 1917, Reina-Valera 1909, 和合本, 文語訳 and Bíblia Portuguesa Mundial.
