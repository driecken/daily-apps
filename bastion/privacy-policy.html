Privacy Policy

This policy covers the iOS app Bastion (the "App"), published by Dana Riecken. Last updated: October 24, 2026

We don't collect your data

The App has no accounts, advertising, analytics, crash reporting or tracking, and it doesn't use third-party SDKs. The developer runs no server and receives none of your information.

Checks run on your device

Your security score, checklist, history, reminder settings and any API key you enter are stored only on your device (the API key in the iOS Keychain). Score data is shared with the App's widget through on-device storage. Deleting the App deletes this data.

Network requests you start

  • Password breach check: the password is hashed on your device and only the first 5 characters of its SHA-1 hash are sent to the Pwned Passwords service (api.pwnedpasswords.com). The password itself never leaves your device.
  • Email breach lookup (optional): if you add your own Have I Been Pwned API key, the email address you type is sent to haveibeenpwned.com to look it up.
  • Website scanner and network check: the domain you enter is contacted directly and resolved through Cloudflare DNS (cloudflare-dns.com); the App also contacts www.apple.com to test connectivity.
  • iOS version check: the App downloads the list of current iOS versions from endoflife.date.

These services receive standard request information such as your IP address under their own privacy policies.

Face ID and Touch ID

The App only checks whether biometrics are set up. It never accesses or stores biometric data.

Notifications

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device.

In-app purchases

Bastion Pro is processed entirely by Apple. The developer receives no personal or payment information.

Children

The App collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
