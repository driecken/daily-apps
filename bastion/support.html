Frequently asked questions

How is my score calculated? Bastion checks for a passcode, Face ID or Touch ID, an up-to-date iOS version, and signs of a jailbreak. Each check is weighted and combined into a score from 0 to 100.

Does Bastion see my passwords? No. Passwords are hashed on your phone, and only the first 5 characters of the hash are sent for the breach check.

Why can't Bastion tell me whether my Wi-Fi is encrypted? iOS doesn't let apps read that. Check Settings › Wi-Fi for a "Weak Security" or "Unsecured Network" warning.

How do I use email breach lookup? It requires your own Have I Been Pwned API key. Add it in Settings › Advanced.

How do I restore Bastion Pro? Open the Pro screen and tap Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple ID.

How do I get a refund? Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
